## sfw
Safe for all audiences. Modest clothing exactly as described. No suggestive poses, no suggestive framing, no exposed underwear, no fan-service angles.

## no-text
No text, letters, numbers, captions, signatures or logos anywhere in the image.

## no-watermark
No watermark, no frame, no border, no UI elements, no stamp.

## plain-background
Plain, flat, uncluttered, very light near-white background with a faint lavender tint, not grey and not pure white, with no scenery, no gradient and no shadows on the ground, so the white sticker outline reads clearly and the figure can be cut out cleanly.

## clean-colour
Bright, clean, saturated colour with crisp line art. No grey haze, no fog, no dull or washed-out filter and no grey colour cast over the image.

## framing
Each full-body figure is drawn large, filling most of the canvas height with only a small even margin on every side; nothing is cropped, not even the ears or the hair; the camera is level and straight on unless the pose says otherwise.

## references
The attached images are approved references for the same characters. Keep the same face shape, eye style, ear shape, hair, outfit details, line weight and colour palette as the references.

## style-reference
The attached image is a style reference only: it shows a design sheet of a different character. Match its sticker outline, line art, shading, colour treatment, proportions style and sheet layout. Do not copy that character's hair, outfit, rabbit ears or face; draw the character described above, with her own hair, outfit, ears and face.

## sheet-order
The first attached image is character 1's approved design sheet and the second attached image is character 2's approved design sheet. Draw each character to match her own sheet exactly: face, hair, ears, outfit and colours. Do not mix details between the two characters.

## no-human-ears
No human ears: the only ears are the rabbit ears on top of the head; the sides of the head where human ears would be are covered by hair.

## no-human-ears-edit
Unless the instruction explicitly says otherwise: No human ears: the only ears are the rabbit ears on top of the head; the sides of the head where human ears would be are covered by hair. If human ears show there, remove them and extend the hair over that area.

## pixel-framing
The subject is centred and fills most of the canvas with only a small even margin; the ears, hair and tail are never cut off by the canvas edge unless the framing above says otherwise; the camera is level and straight on.

The illustrated line weight, shading and outline are replaced by the pixel-art rules above. Draw only the one character described above; the other character in the line-up must not appear.

## master-reference
The third attached image is the character's approved pixel-art master in front view. Match its pixel size, outline colours, palette, shading and sticker edge exactly, so the new picture looks like the same artist drew it; do not copy its pose, which is replaced by the pose described above.

## prop-framing
The object is centred and fills most of the canvas with only a small even margin; nothing is cropped by the canvas edge; it is drawn straight on, with no scenery, no ground and no cast shadow.

## scene-framing
Both characters are drawn together as one group on one canvas, each complete from the tips of the ears to the feet, with nothing cropped by the canvas edge. The camera is level, and the group is centred unless the composition line says otherwise. Character 1 is clearly taller than character 2, as in the line-up.
