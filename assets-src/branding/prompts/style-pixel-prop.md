Pixel art style: detailed pixel art in the spirit of late 16-bit console games, drawn on a strict square pixel grid.
Outlines: coloured outlines in the darkest shade of the local colour, never black; selective outlining where shapes meet.
Palette: a small locked palette of 4 to 8 colours; hue-shifted ramps, with shadows shifting toward violet or blue and highlights shifting toward warm yellow.
Clusters: clean pixel clusters, no orphan pixels, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no sub-pixel detail; dithering only for large gradients.
Sticker edge (an important trait): an unbroken white outer outline one grid pixel wide around the whole silhouette, with a soft lavender-grey rim one grid pixel wide outside it.
