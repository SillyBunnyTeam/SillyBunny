## asymmetric
The left ear is long and slightly rumpled, with a soft kink partway up; the right ear is shorter and floppier, bending over at the tip. The two ears are clearly different in length and posture, and the composition is balanced without being symmetric.

## symmetric
The two ears are a mirrored pair: the same length and the same slightly rumpled shape, one the reflection of the other about a vertical centre line, so the whole emblem is symmetric left to right.
