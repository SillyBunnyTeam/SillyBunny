## asymmetric
The left ear is long and slightly rumpled, with a soft kink partway up; the right ear is shorter and floppier, bending over at the tip so the carrot can hang from it. The two ears are clearly different in length and posture, and the composition is balanced without being symmetric.

## symmetric
The two ears are a mirrored pair: the same length and the same slightly rumpled shape, one the reflection of the other about the vertical centre line, each bending over a little at the tip. The brain and the ears are symmetric left to right; the two charms are the only difference between the sides.
