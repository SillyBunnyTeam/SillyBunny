Illustration style: clean, bright digital anime-style character art with cel shading and crisp line art, drawn with confident creative flair and a charming, appealing, slightly stylised design.
Sticker outline (the most important trait): a thick, bold, unbroken white outline of even width runs around the entire silhouette of every figure, tracing every hair strand, both rabbit ears and the tail, about 2.5 to 3% of the figure height wide, with a thin soft lavender-grey rim just outside the white outline, like a die-cut vinyl sticker. The outline never thins, never breaks and never disappears anywhere.
Line art: smooth medium-weight outer contours in very dark warm brown or plum, never pure black; thinner interior lines; hair lines tinted toward the hair colour; tapered strand tips.
Shading: base tone plus one shadow tone with slightly feathered edges; gentle top-to-bottom gradient washes on hair and fabric; an occasional darker accent in deep folds; no texture and no painterly brushwork.
Highlights: soft sheen bands on hair; small glossy specular dots on eyes, buttons and shoes; no heavy rim light.
Eyes: large, glossy, vertical-oval irises, darker at the top and brighter at the bottom, two or three white catchlights, a thick upper lash line with a flicked outer corner, a red-tinted lower lash line.
Face: tiny or omitted nose; small mouth; soft pink airbrushed cheek blush, sometimes with fine diagonal hatch lines.
Skin: warm light skin with a pink flush at knees, elbows, knuckles and fingertips.
Proportions: slender, slightly long legs, small hands with tapered fingers.
Hair: soft volume built from clumped strands with gradient shading, with a single stray strand as an accent.
Rabbit ears: the ears are a primary means of expression and change shape with the emotion: standing straight up when alert, bending at the middle when relaxed, drooping backwards when sad, splaying or shooting up when surprised, tilting when curious. White outside with pink-red inner surfaces and soft shading.
Colour: clean and bright. Saturated character colours (hair, eyes, tie) with fresh, clear clothing colours; luminous pastel values; shadows in soft violet or blue, never grey; no grey haze, no dull or washed-out filter and no overall colour cast; no pure black and no pure white inside the figure.
Posing: gentle and characterful, slight head tilt, contrapposto, hands doing something such as clasped, waving or holding a prop.
Finish: clean digital rendering; soft glow only in backgrounds, never on the figure.
No human ears: the only ears are the rabbit ears on top of the head; the sides of the head where human ears would be are covered by hair.
