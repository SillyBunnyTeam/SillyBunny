Character design sheet on a plain, flat, very light near-white background with a faint lavender tint, landscape orientation, laid out as a clean grid with even spacing.
Each figure and each face panel is its own die-cut sticker: a thick, bold, unbroken white outline of even width, about 2.5 to 3% of the figure height, traces the entire silhouette, including every hair strand, both ears and the tail, with a thin soft lavender-grey rim outside it.
Top row: the same character drawn full body three times at equal scale, each figure large enough to fill most of the panel height with only small margins, in a relaxed neutral standing pose with the arms slightly away from the body so the outfit is visible: front view, three-quarter view, back view.
Middle row: three close-up face panels of the same character at equal scale, each showing a clearly different ear pose that matches the expression:
neutral face, ears relaxed in their natural resting pose;
gentle smile, ears perked up and tilted slightly forward;
surprised face, ears shot straight up and splayed apart.
Bottom row: six plain square colour swatches for the character's main colours (hair, eyes and the main outfit colours), with no labels.
